	ex02:
Requires making pairs of numbers, or pairs of pairs
The only relevant number is the last number of the pair
 -> no "physical" pair is needed, as long as recursion level is known.

	r = recursion

	r0 = 1: one number
	r1 = 2: pair of numbers
	r2 = 4: pair of pairs
	r3 = 8: pair of pairs of pairs

	Size of one pair
		(2^r)

	(max) value of pair n
		data[n * (2^r) - 1]

	total pairs
		container.size() / (2^r)
